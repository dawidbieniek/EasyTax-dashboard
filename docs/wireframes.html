EasyTax — wireframes · reference

Dark mode. Screens: 1 Dashboard · 2 Client page (3 tabs) · 3 Clients list · 4 Add client · 5 Settings. Avatars are WinUI PersonPicture (photo, or initials when none). The left rail is a collapsed NavigationView. Dashed blue outlines mark layout containers, not visible UI.

1 · Dashboard

App bar: avatar + client picker (type to search), previous/next, year, month strip (green = accounted, orange = not yet, grey = before the client joined), and the accounted toggle for the selected month. Body: three gauges on top; below, charts stacked on the left and the month's inputs, checklist and notes on the right. Both columns end at the same line — the notes card absorbs any extra height.

2 · Client page

Same app bar as the dashboard without the month-scoped controls (year, months, accounted toggle), so switching clients behaves identically. The header band carries the key facts at a glance: photo (with change/remove), name, contact line, chips for tax setup and relief status, and a small stats column. Edit makes the tab forms editable; Save/Cancel then appear in the app bar. Tab content lives in a container capped at ~920 px, left-aligned with the header; fields are laid out in cards (two columns), which collapse to one column below ~760 px via an AdaptiveTrigger.
